Feature guides

The features that do the worrying for you

Four tools most bulk editors don’t have — and how to get the most out of each one.

Scheduled sales with verified rollback

Schedule a sale with a start time and an end time. When it ends, VerifiedBulk restores every price, re-reads every product, and emails you a receipt. If anything needs attention, the email says exactly what.

✓ Revert fired Jul 2, 11:00 PM
1,204 / 1,204 prices restored and verified
Receipt sent to you@store.com

Filters re-evaluate when the sale fires — products added to your Sale collection after scheduling are included automatically. Schedules show in your store’s timezone.

Conflict-aware undo

Undo never blindly overwrites. If a price changed after your edit — by you, your team, or another app — that item is flagged instead of restored, and you see all three values side by side.

Wool Beanie — price
before edit $18.00 · our edit $14.40 · now $12.99
→ Skip (keep $12.99) or Force restore ($18.00)

Scheduled rollbacks follow the same rule while you sleep: clean items restore, conflicted items wait for you. Nothing is force-written without your say-so.

The Recycle Bin

Bulk deletion with a 30-day safety net. Before anything is deleted, VerifiedBulk snapshots the full product — variants, prices, images, tags, metafields — and copies images into your store’s own Files so they survive.

Deleted Jun 30 · Filtered by tag “discontinued”
14 products · restorable for 23 more days

Honest limits: restored products get new IDs, inventory restores best-effort to one location, and analytics history doesn’t come back. The restore report tells you exactly what did and didn’t.

Archive instead

Most of the time you don’t need to delete at all. Archiving hides products from your storefront, keeps every ID and stat intact, and reverses instantly. VerifiedBulk offers it first, every time you reach for delete.

Rule of thumb: archive for “maybe later,” Recycle Bin for “actually gone.” Both run through the same preview-and-verify loop.

Playbook

Habits of merchants who never lose data

Small practices that compound. Steal all eight.

  • Read the preview count, not just the rows. The total tells you the true blast radius before you commit — if it surprises you, your filter is wrong.
  • Schedule the revert with the sale. Set start and end together and the rollback can’t be forgotten. The receipt email confirms it worked.
  • Trust a zero-match block. If find & replace matches nothing, VerifiedBulk blocks the run. That’s not an error — it just saved you a no-op job.
  • Archive before you delete. If there’s any “maybe” in the decision, archive. Delete is for certainty; the Recycle Bin is the backstop, not the plan.
  • Resolve conflicts same-day. Conflicted items are waiting on your judgment. The three-value view makes it a ten-second decision per item.
  • Use tags as staging. Tag a set first, review it in Shopify admin, then run your real edit filtered by that tag. Two small jobs beat one risky one.
  • Before big sales, test on ten. Run your BFCM price change on a ten-product filter first. Verify the receipt, then scale the same edit definition to everything.
  • Let old history prune. Snapshots expire with your plan’s window (7 or 90 days). Undo what needs undoing this week — don’t treat history as a backup system.

FAQ

Quick answers

What does “verified” actually mean?

After every job, VerifiedBulk re-reads each product it touched straight from Shopify and compares the live value to what it intended to write. Matches are marked verified; mismatches are itemized with reasons and a one-click retry. You never have to take “success” on faith.

Is the free plan actually usable?

Yes — it’s every feature, capped at 50 products per edit with 7 days of undo history. Verified edits, scheduled rollbacks, conflict-aware undo, and the Recycle Bin all included. Small catalogs can run entirely on it.

What does Pro add?

Pro is $19/month flat: unlimited products per edit and 90 days of undo history. No task counting, ever — run one job a month or a hundred, the price is the price.

What happens if my scheduled rollback hits a conflict at 3am?

Clean items are restored and verified automatically. Conflicted items are never force-written while you sleep — they’re flagged, and your receipt email says “N items need your review” with a link straight to the resolution screen.

What doesn’t the Recycle Bin restore?

Restored products come back with new product IDs (old links and integrations pointing at the old ID won’t follow), inventory restores best-effort to a single location, automatic collections recalculate on their own, and sales history isn’t part of a product object. The restore report states exactly what came back.

What data does VerifiedBulk store?

Snapshots of the product fields you edit (that’s what powers undo and the Recycle Bin) and your shop’s notification email. Customer data isn’t touched beyond Shopify’s required privacy webhooks, which we honor automatically.

How do I get help?

Email support@verifiedbulk.app — it goes straight to the developer. Include the job’s verification report if your question is about a specific edit; it usually contains the answer.
